WHICH WATER IS THIS?PRIMARY COOLANT. Yellow: hot-leg; cyan: cold-leg. The two loops are separate. Saturation boundary from the Wagner–Pruss correlation; saturation temperature by numerical inversion.
1 · SUBCOOLED LIQUIDBelow the saturation temperature at current pressure, the primary coolant remains liquid. Reference point ≈ 315 °C / 15.5 MPa.
2 · CRITICAL POINTAbout 373.946 °C and 22.064 MPa. Above both coordinates there is no distinct liquid–vapor boundary.
MODEL LIMITSTracks bulk coolant only. No fuel temperature, cladding temperature, DNB, DNBR, or vessel stress. Relief-valve and pressure limits are game parameters.
Supercritical water is a fluid state, not plasma or fusion.
